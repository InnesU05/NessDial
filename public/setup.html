NessDial Call Forwarding Setup Guide

To get the most out of your NessDial agent, you need to connect your existing business phone number to your new AI number.

How it works: You do not need to port your number or change providers. Instead, you will use a standard telecom feature called "Conditional Call Forwarding". This tells your phone provider: "If I don't answer my phone after 15 seconds, or if I decline the call, forward the caller to my NessDial AI number."

📱 For Mobile Devices (iPhone & Android)

Note: While some phones have forwarding settings in their menus, those settings often forward ALL calls immediately. To forward ONLY missed calls, the most reliable method across all networks is using "Carrier Codes".

Method 1: The Carrier Code Method (Recommended for UK Networks)

Simply open your phone's keypad (where you normally dial a number to make a call) and type in your network's specific code, followed by your new AI Number, then press the Call button.

  • EE / O2 / Vodafone / Three (Standard UK Networks):
    Dial **61*[Your AI Number]*11*15# and press Call.
    (The "15" at the end tells it to ring for 15 seconds before the AI picks up. You can change this to 10 or 20 if you prefer).

If the code above fails, please Google: "How to set up conditional call forwarding on [Your Specific Network]" for their exact dial code.

Method 2: Android Menu Setting (Samsung, Google Pixel, etc.)

Depending on your carrier, you may be able to do this directly in your Android settings:

  1. Open the Phone app.
  2. Tap the 3 dots (menu) in the top right corner and select Settings.
  3. Tap Supplementary Services (or "Calling Accounts" > "Call Forwarding").
  4. Select Call Forwarding > Voice Calls.
  5. Tap Forward when unanswered (and/or "Forward when busy").
  6. Enter your new NessDial AI Number and tap Turn On.

☎️ For VoIP & Landline Systems (BT, RingCentral, 8x8, etc.)

If you use a cloud phone system or a digital landline for your business, you will need to apply this in your provider's dashboard:

  1. Log in to your provider's admin dashboard on your computer.
  2. Go to your user settings, often labeled "Call Routing", "Call Handling", or "Extensions".
  3. Look for the rules regarding "Unanswered Calls", "Missed Calls", or "Call Forwarding".
  4. Select "Forward to an external number" and input your new NessDial AI Number.
  5. Set the ringing duration to 15 or 20 seconds before the forward happens.
CRITICAL - CANCELLING YOUR SERVICE:
If you ever cancel your NessDial subscription, you MUST disable call forwarding on your device before we deactivate your agent. To disable conditional call forwarding on most UK networks, dial ##61# and press Call. If you do not do this, your customers will hear a dead line when you miss their calls.

Need help?
If you are struggling to find the setting, the easiest solution is to call your telecom provider's customer service line and say: "Hi, I need to set up conditional call forwarding so that my missed calls and unanswered calls forward to [Your AI Number]." They can usually turn it on for you in a few minutes.
